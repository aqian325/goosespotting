About this project

This deck is part of April Qian’s MFA thesis in information design — an exploratory fieldwork tool for noticing traces of the more-than-human urban world. It invites you to slow down, notice, and reflect through close observation of the traces that geese leave behind. Through observation, mapping, creative response, and sensory attunement, you’ll explore how civic systems, community life, and material presences shape — and are shaped by — our nonhuman neighbors.

Draw cards in any order, following your curiosity. Tap to flip. Respond by writing, drawing, photographing, or simply noticing. Take care to observe a respectful distance from the geese.

These cards are for solo wandering, or for sharing quietly with a friend.

Take your time. Notice differently.
